Well, Whale

Maya Arnold Adobe Substance
Before After

Concept:

The title is a playful twist on the English expression "Well, well...", replacing the second well with "whale" to create a visual and phonetic pun.

This artwork captures the quiet grandeur of the underwater world through a deep blue-green color palette that envelops the entire composition. Beams of light descending from the surface evoke a sense of nature's sacred presence, illuminating the vast ocean with a calm, almost spiritual atmosphere.

At its core, the piece tells a story of the bond between a mother whale and her calf. The composition is carefully arranged, with the mother swimming ahead and above while the calf follows closely behind at a safe distance. Together, they move toward the same destination, symbolizing guidance, protection, and companionship. The dark silhouettes of coral reefs and marine plants in the foreground frame the scene, emphasizing the graceful forms of the whales against the immense openness of the sea.

Skills Developed:

  • Creating an Underwater Atmosphere: Capturing the essence of the deep ocean.
  • Lighting from Above the Water: Simulating light filtering down from the surface of the water to the ocean floor.
  • Modeling Creatures with Low Poly Count: Designing creatures without excessive faces or polygons.
  • Creating the Underwater Environment: Creating realistic underwater conditions.

Well, Whale was an experimental project for me, focusing on lighting and creating an atmosphere in an underwater landscape, which was a new challenge.

Unspoken

Maya Character Acting

Concept:

Unspoken is a character acting study created to explore subtle emotional performance through facial expressions and body language. The project focuses on eye direction, facial nuance, breathing, posture, and timing to deliver a believable performance.

The objective was to strengthen the fundamentals of acting for animation while communicating emotion with clarity and authenticity.
